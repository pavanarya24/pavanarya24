## What I do

I trade and I build the tools I trade with. My process is deliberately simple: opening-range breakouts and 9 EMA / VWAP setups, filtered by order flow and dealer gamma exposure levels. Every idea is tested walk-forward and with Monte Carlo before it touches capital, and ideas that stop working are retired.

Top 7 of about 9,000 traders in the fastbull.com forex and gold competition (April 2025).

## What I build

- **Research engine:** hypothesis-driven platform with pre-registered experiments, walk-forward and Monte Carlo validation, data-integrity checks and a feature registry.
- **Order-flow platform:** tick-data pipeline on DuckDB with Lee-Ready trade classification, volume delta, footprint and cluster engines, and a PySide6 terminal.
- **Tape simulator and replay engine:** tick-by-tick Level 2 / Time & Sales reconstruction for execution training and edge research.
- **Prop-firm risk engine:** NQ/MNQ opening-range-breakout system with daily-loss, trailing-drawdown and position-sizing constraints, tested across a 432-configuration parameter sweep.
- **Signal tools:** XAUUSD signal terminal (Python / Streamlit), Pine Script v6 candle-reversal model built as a finite-state machine, and a real-time WebSocket scanner for lead-lag cross-correlation across a ~200-stock universe.
- **Reporting pipeline:** parser for Fidelity trade confirmations with FIFO lot matching for client P&L attribution.
